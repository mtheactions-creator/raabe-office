# Night run 2026-11-08/09: chart formatting, every option by hand, the remaining open items

The owner tried RaabeCalc's chart formatting and found it "downright broken in many parts": the value axis would not
draw, and there was no way to choose the data series. They asked to rebuild it with thorough testing "by hand", to do
everything else still open, and to check whether all options in all apps work when triggered by hand. They left the PC
with mouse and keyboard to us until 07:00.

**Why the earlier checks missed it.** They drove the chart model through test APIs and compared numbers (axis scales),
but never clicked a control and looked at the drawing. Tonight every check drives the real release exe the way a student
does:
- trusted mouse and key events at the control's position (through DevTools, invisible);
- passes with the real OS mouse under the real-input lock.

The result of each option is compared with Office doing the same, and Office opens our saved file.

The work ran in agents on their own branches, merged into main one by one. Each merge passed the type check, all unit
tests, check-chars and the anonymize check. Office ran only in processes of our own.
