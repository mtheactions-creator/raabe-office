## What was built

1. **Second screen, video, GIF, windows (D-074).** The show on the other monitor in a borderless full-screen
   window made by the native side, the Referentenansicht in the main window (timer with pause and restart, tools,
   next slide, notes with text size, Anzeigedauern swap and duplicate); one page draws both, so keys, ink and the
   laser work on either screen. The virtual display driver (D-073) was installed with the owner's confirmation and
   is switched off between tests. Video erstellen (4K to 480p, our MP4 writer with H.264 and AAC from the WebView's
   encoders, PowerPoint's measured timeline) and Animiertes GIF erstellen (our GIF writer). Alle anordnen and
   Überlappend as frames inside the window. Verblassen durch Schwarz is read and drawn.
2. **Formeloptionen and drawing (D-075, D-076).** PowerPoint's Formeloptionen dialog; Strg+C/X inside an equation
   copies MathML or the linear format. The 40 text warps from ECMA-376's definitions, drawn letter by letter; text
   gradients; Form formatieren > Effekte (Schatten, Spiegelung, Leuchten, Weiche Kanten, 3D-Format, 3D-Drehung),
   reached from the Formeffekte menus' "…optionen..."; cell bevels drawn (not yet measured).
3. **SmartArt (D-077).** Einfügen > SmartArt with PowerPoint's categories and 146 layouts, In SmartArt
   konvertieren, SmartArt-Design (Form hinzufügen, Textbereich with levels, Höher/Tiefer stufen, Verschieben,
   Layouts, Farben ändern, Grafik zurücksetzen). Our parts carry PowerPoint's ids, so PowerPoint lays our diagrams
   out with its own layouts; RaabePresent draws them from PowerPoint's measured arrangements (1 to 8 items).
4. **Own content and Aufzeichnen (D-078).** 19 transition sounds and 24 textures of our own (generated by scripts
   in the repository), six designs with artwork of our own, the Aufzeichnen tab (record with microphone and
   camera per slide, narrations and times written as PowerPoint stores them, Neue Anzeigedauern testen,
   Aufzeichnung löschen, Bildschirmaufzeichnung, Audio). The app's CSP now allows media and fetch from blob: and
   the app's own files (before, media from blob: could not have played in the real app).
5. **Freihandgleichung and macros (D-079).** Windows' Math Input Panel recognises handwriting, our MathML reader
   brings the equation in. @raabe/vba: the VBA project format read and written and our interpreter; in
   RaabePresent the message bar, Ansicht > Makros, our editor, Makro ausführen as a click action in the show, a
   PowerPoint-like object model.

## Decisions taken on my own (for the owner to confirm or overturn)

1. Sounds generated, not downloaded (the owner left it open; licence-free and reproducible). (D-078)
2. The virtual display driver from VirtualDrivers/Virtual-Display-Driver (MIT, signed), installed with a small
   script of ours (devcon's two steps); switched off with Windows' own "Nur PC-Bildschirm" after each test. (D-073)
3. With two screens, a right-click on the audience's screen opens its menu in the Referentenansicht. (D-074)
4. Video: only MP4 (PowerPoint also offers WMV); GIF at 15 frames per second. (D-074)
5. Alle anordnen inside our window (we keep presentations as tabs, PowerPoint has windows). (D-074)
6. Formeloptionen: PowerPoint's smaller dialog, kept as this user's settings with RaabeWrite's keys. (D-075)
7. Warped text: our letters are placed and scaled one by one, PowerPoint bends their outlines; while edited the
   text shows plain. (D-076)
8. Effekte pane: values written into lon as entered; COM's RotationX counts the other way (measured). (D-076)
9. SmartArt: PowerPoint's arrangements for 1 to 8 items stored as measured numbers (845 KB), families beyond;
   our layout/quick style/colours parts are minimal and carry PowerPoint's ids. (D-077)
10. SmartArt's Farben ändern: the main colour sets by PowerPoint's names and ids; Schnellformatvorlagen and Von
    rechts nach links not built yet. (D-077)
11. Recording: one take per slide; ink drawn while recording is not kept as an animation. (D-078)
12. Freihandgleichung through Windows' own Math Input Panel (no recogniser of our own yet, so not on the iPad).
    (D-079)
13. Macros: written projects carry no compiled code (Office compiles the source); endless loops stop after 5
    million steps; no UserForms or New objects yet. (D-079)
