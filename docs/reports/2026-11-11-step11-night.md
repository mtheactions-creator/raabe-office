# Night run 2026-11-11/12: Step 11, the app around the document like Office

The owner's first test of the 2026-11-09 night run found four things in RaabeCalc: the context menu stayed open on the
next click into a cell, chart selection marks looked different from Excel's, the chart Format pane showed text where
Excel shows pictures (and, they thought, a different order), and the font colour icon had a confusing middle bar. Their
verdict: "Those are things that we need to get right in all three apps and in different variations." Step 11's scope
(docs/HANDOVER.md) turns that into five parts plus a sweep for pictures where Office has pictures. The owner gave the go
in the evening and left the PC with mouse and keyboard to us until morning; the run stops when the 5-hour usage window
that is open at 07:00 reaches 65 %.

**How it was measured.** Every part was recorded in our own Office instances first (COM, UI Automation, screenshots of
our own windows, real input under the lock), then built, then compared automatically by a check that stays in
`tools/feel-lab`. Our side runs the release exes with trusted DevTools input.

## Found on the way

- The Zellenformatvorlagen gallery (and every popup without a position of its own) opened at the bottom of the window
  instead of under its button. Fixed in `showPopup` for all three apps.
- RaabeWrite's Schriftfarbe started with #FF0000; Word's "Rot" is #EE0000 (measured).
- The ribbon took the keyboard focus on a mouse press; Office's never does.
- PowerPoint's probe pressed a second Esc on a ScreenTip and recorded a deselect that PowerPoint does not do. The probe
  now parks the mouse before reading the state; noted in D-158 for future probes.
