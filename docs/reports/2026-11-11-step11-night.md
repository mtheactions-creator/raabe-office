# Night run 2026-11-11/12: Step 11, the app around the document like Office

The owner's first test of the 2026-11-09 night run found four things in RaabeCalc: the context menu stayed open on the
next click into a cell, chart selection marks looked different from Excel's, the chart Format pane showed text where
Excel shows pictures (and, they thought, sections in a different order), and the font colour icon had a confusing middle
bar. Their verdict: "Those are things that we need to get right in all three apps and in different variations." Step
11's scope (docs/HANDOVER.md) turns that into five parts plus a sweep for pictures wherever Office has pictures. The
owner gave the go in the evening and left the PC with mouse and keyboard to us until morning.

**How it was measured.** Every part was recorded in our own Office instances first (COM, UI Automation, crops of our
own windows, real input under the lock), then built, then compared by a check that stays in `tools/feel-lab`. Our side
runs the release exes with trusted DevTools input. The final numbers below were all taken on main after the last merge
(e749da0 and the commit of this report), with the release exes rebuilt from it.

## Found on the way

- The Zellenformatvorlagen gallery (and every popup without a position of its own) opened at the bottom of the window
  instead of under its button. Fixed in `showPopup` for all three apps.
- The ribbon took the keyboard focus on a mouse press; Office's never does. A Fett click while the slide thumbnails had
  the focus moved it away in RaabePresent.
- RaabeWrite's Schriftfarbe started with #FF0000; Word's "Rot" is #EE0000 (measured).
- Our widest ribbon layouts were Office's first reductions: on a maximized window at the owner's resolution Office shows
  labels and large buttons that ours did not. Now derived and measured (D-159).
- Office breaks some large-button labels at fixed points ("Seiten-ränder", "Druck-bereich"); ours now does the same.
- PowerPoint's probe pressed a second Esc on a ScreenTip and recorded a deselect that PowerPoint does not do; fixed in
  the probe and noted in D-158 for future probes. Word's open dropdowns take the foreground even off screen (D-165).
