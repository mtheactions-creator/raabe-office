# Step 10 report: open items, macros, drag test, look corpus (2026-11-08)

The owner agreed on the plan (D-080) and left the PC to us until it was finished. The plan had four parts:
- **A:** the open items of RaabeCalc and RaabePresent, Step 7b (charts in RaabeWrite) and macros in RaabeCalc.
- **B:** extensive macro tests against Excel and PowerPoint, using our own versions of popular macros.
- **C:** a drag test in all three apps, under the rule "no sudden, unwanted jumps".
- **D:** a look corpus: files made in Office must look the same when opened in RaabeX.

This report lists what was built, what was measured and how, and what is still open. All Office work ran in Office
processes of our own (invisible through COM, or with real input under the real-input lock). No security setting, no
Trust Center option and no Office user name was changed. RaabePresent's work was done by its own session on its
own branch (see the end).

## What was built

1. **Excel's remaining features (part A, D-081 to D-093).**
   - Structured references, calculated columns and growing tables; LET, LAMBDA and its helpers; ZELLE and INFO.
   - Seitenlayout > Designs; the Szenario-Manager and Konsolidieren; the Sparkline tab; every chart type except the
     map; Bildformat; Spezialfilter.
   - Datenschnitt, a simple local Daten abrufen, the Prognoseblatt with PROGNOSE.ETS.
   - Zeichnen, the whole Fenster group (Teilen, Neues Fenster …) and the workbook views.
   - Every one of these was recorded first in our own Excel and compared both ways.
2. **Charts in RaabeWrite (Step 7b, D-088, D-091).** Word's charts are drawn by the shared chart engine.
   Einfügen > Diagramm and Daten bearbeiten write charts that Word opens and can edit.
3. **Office's cloud fonts (D-089).** Aptos, Aptos Narrow and Aptos Display come from Office's own font cache, so
   documents set in them look as in Office.
4. **Macros in RaabeCalc (D-095).**
   - .xlsm files open and save; the macros run in the shared VBA interpreter against Excel's object model.
   - Ansicht > Makros (Alt+F8) and the security message bar ("Inhalt aktivieren"); one undo step per run.
   - Saving as .xlsx asks first, as Excel does.
   - The Excel comparison found two faults in the shared VBA package, now fixed for both apps:
     - The project's language ID made Excel read `Range.Formula` as German.
     - Dates were off by the time zone.
5. **Printing like Excel (D-094).**
   - Excel prints charts and shapes and uses printer metrics for columns and rows; RaabeCalc now does both.
   - Borders, gridlines, headers and footers and page centring follow Excel's measurements.
   - All 26 corpus workbooks have Excel's page count.
6. **Drag test (D-096).** Every drag anchor is dragged with real input along ten pointer paths. At every frame we
   check that the dragged thing follows the mouse and never leaps. Esc and Strg+Z are checked as Office does them;
   this was recorded first: in Excel, Esc does not cancel a border or fill drag, but it does put back a shape or a
   chart. Fixed:
   - A real jump of the zoom slider in all apps.
   - Shapes in RaabeCalc that did not move at all when dragged before being selected.
   - Esc behaviour in both apps as in Office.

## Decisions taken on my own (as the owner allowed)

- **Power Query and Power Pivot:** a simple local import now (from a workbook, from Text/CSV, as a table on a new
  sheet as Excel 365 does). The query editor, refresh and cloud sources come later (D-080, D-092).
- **Macro tests in Excel:** Excel's copy of each test macro gets a helper module that answers MsgBox and InputBox
  from a list and catches errors. A dialog in the hidden Excel would wait forever, and we do not click in Office.
- **PROGNOSE.ETS:** built on the published method. Excel's parameter search is not documented: the forecasts are
  0.4 % off Excel's on average, the confidence intervals more (D-092).
- **Force push:** the history clean-up still waits for your OK (D-080). Nothing was force-pushed.
